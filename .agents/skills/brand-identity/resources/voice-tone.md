# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating text, adhere to this brand persona.

## Brand Personality Keywords
* **Speed-focused**: Emphasize speed and closing the gap between thought and action ("150 WPM", "Save 20+ hours a month").
* **Privacy-first**: Reassure users about security ("Zero data retention", "Private by design", "Stored on your device").
* **Seamless & Effortless**: Highlight an uninterrupted workflow ("Glide through work", "Without breaking flow").
* **Empowering & Direct**: Focus on what the user can achieve ("Start free, upgrade to fly", "Your thoughts move faster than your hands").

## Grammar & Mechanics rules
* **Headings:** Use Title Case for main headings (H1, H2). Use sentence case for subheadings (H3+).
* **Punctuation:** Avoid exclamation points (!) in standard interface copy. Use periods for complete sentences.
* **Clarity:** Prefer active voice over passive voice. Keep sentences short and punchy to reflect the speed of the product.

## Terminology Guide

| Do Not Use | Use Instead |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Transcription app" | "The voice layer for every app" |
| "Type" or "Keyboarding" | "Speak" or "Voice" |
| "We store your data securely" | "Zero data retention" / "Stored on your device" |
| "Raw text" | "Clean, formatted text" / "AI auto-edits" |
