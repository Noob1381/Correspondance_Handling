# Email Drafting Protocol

Whenever I ask you to draft a response or write an email on behalf of a specific business (e.g., Business_A, Business_B), you MUST automatically execute the following workflow:

1. **Context Loading**: Immediately read the contents of the `business_samples/[Business Name]` folder. This includes reading their tone of voice guidelines, past email samples, and any official rules or regulations (PDFs or links).
2. **Brand Consistency**: Apply the `brand-identity` and `managing-brands` skills to ensure the response strictly adheres to their specific brand persona.
3. **Compliance Check**: Verify that the drafted response complies with the official rules and standards provided in their folder.
4. **Humanize Text**: Apply the `humanizing-text` skill to strip out any robotic, "AI-sounding" language, ensuring the final output reads naturally and empathetically.

Do this automatically without me having to explicitly tell you to use these skills or look in these folders.
