# Official Regulations & Compliance Standards
**McLean Strategic Solutions**

As a principal consultant managing environmental approvals and audits across multiple Australian states and territories (WA, Qld, NSW, SA, NT), all correspondence and documentation must comply with the following overarching and state-specific frameworks. Cite a specific act, regulator, or standard only when the correspondence concerns it.

> Last reviewed: TBC. Legislation and regulator names change; confirm they are current before relying on them in client correspondence.

## 1. National Framework
- **Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act 1999 (EPBC Act):** Federal legislation applying to projects with significant impacts on matters of national environmental significance.

## 2. State & Territory Regulations

### Western Australia (WA)
- **Legislation:** *Environmental Protection Act 1986*
- **Regulatory Body:** Department of Water and Environmental Regulation (DWER)
- **Key Focus:** Prescribed activities, native vegetation clearing, and emissions/discharges.

### Queensland (Qld)
- **Legislation:** *Environmental Protection Act 1994*
- **Regulatory Body:** Department of Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation (DETSI)
- **Key Focus:** Environmental Authorities (EA) for Environmentally Relevant Activities (ERAs).

### New South Wales (NSW)
- **Legislation:** *Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997* (POEO Act)
- **Regulatory Body:** NSW Environment Protection Authority (EPA)
- **Key Focus:** Adherence to State Environmental Planning Policies (SEPPs) and environment protection licences.

### South Australia (SA)
- **Legislation:** *Environment Protection Act 1993* & *Local Nuisance and Litter Control Act 2016*
- **Regulatory Body:** SA EPA

### Northern Territory (NT)
- **Legislation:** *Environment Protection Act 2019* & *Environment Protection Regulations 2020*
- **Regulatory Body:** NT Environment Protection Authority (NT EPA)

## 3. Standard Industry Practices
- **CEMPs:** Construction Environmental Management Plans must be developed and audited for site-specific risks (noise, dust, sediment, waste).
- **ISO Standards:** Alignment with **AS/NZS ISO 14001:2016** (Environmental Management Systems).
- **General Environmental Duty:** Acknowledging the overarching duty to take all reasonable measures to prevent environmental harm during civil construction delivery.
