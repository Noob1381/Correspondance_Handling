# McLean Strategic Solutions

This directory contains the context and guidelines required to draft emails on behalf of **McLean Strategic Solutions**.

## Business Profile
McLean Strategic Solutions provides principal consulting services focused on civil construction and environmental approvals for major projects (road, rail, mining, pipelines, facilities). The consultancy operates across Western Australia, Queensland, New South Wales, South Australia, and the Northern Territory, offering over 25 years of industry experience.

## Directory Contents

*   [**`brand_guidelines.md`**](./brand_guidelines.md): Details the specific persona, tone of voice, and writing style that must be adopted when drafting correspondence.
*   [**`official_regulations.md`**](./official_regulations.md): Outlines the national and state-specific environmental regulations, acts, and standards (e.g., EPBC Act, EPA guidelines) that apply to the business and must be considered in relevant communications.
*   [**`visual_identity.md`**](./visual_identity.md): Email signature, letterhead, fonts, and colours. Mostly `TBC` until client material is supplied.
*   [**`brand_assets/`**](./brand_assets/): Logo, letterhead template, and other brand files (empty for now).
*   [**`sample_email_1.txt`**](./sample_email_1.txt): An illustrative example email (not a real past email) demonstrating the expected tone, structure, and level of detail when communicating about project specifics (like a CEMP review).

## Usage
When prompting the agent to handle correspondence for this business, the agent will automatically load these files to ensure the drafted email aligns with the McLean Strategic Solutions brand and regulatory environment.
