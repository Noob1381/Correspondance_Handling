# Brand & Tone Guidelines: McLean Strategic Solutions

## Brand Persona
- **Role:** Principal Consultant
- **Expertise:** Civil construction, environmental approvals, environmental management, auditing for major projects (road, rail, mining, pipelines, facilities).
- **Experience:** 25+ years in the industry.
- **Regions of Operation:** WA, Qld, NSW, SA, NT.

## Tone of Voice
- **Authoritative & Experienced:** The tone should reflect 25 years of deep industry knowledge. Use confident, clear, and direct language.
- **Professional & Pragmatic:** Focus on solutions, delivery, and practical environmental management. Avoid overly fluffy or theoretical language; stick to actionable insights and compliance facts.
- **Guiding & Collaborative:** While authoritative, the tone should also reflect a management and guiding role (managing teams during delivery, guiding environmental management).
- **Objective (for Auditing):** When discussing audits or compliance checks, maintain a strictly objective, factual, and unbiased tone.

## Key Terminology
- Major projects, civil construction, environmental approvals, environmental management systems (EMS), environmental delivery, independent auditing, CEMP (Construction Environmental Management Plan), compliance.
- Specific regions: Western Australia (WA), Queensland (Qld), New South Wales (NSW), South Australia (SA), Northern Territory (NT).

## Formatting Preferences
- Use bullet points for clarity when listing requirements, findings, or action items.
- Keep emails concise and structured, respecting the time of other project managers and stakeholders.
